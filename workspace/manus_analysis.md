# Manus 2.0 reference deconstruction

## Source and method
Reference: supplied Manus 2.0 MP4, downloaded to `reference/manus_intro.mp4`. `ffprobe`: 1920×1080 H.264 at 30 fps, duration 166.464 s (audio 166.464 s). Nine contact sheets in `analysis/manus_batches/` sample each 20-second range at 1 frame/s (final range 160–166 s). The observations below describe visible design and story; contact-sheet stills cannot prove exact transition curves, cut timing, audio sync or motion blur, so those are not asserted as facts. The soundtrack for the NewEra film is separately analyzed in `beat_map.json`.

## Temporal deconstruction (20 s batches)

### 00–20 — Identity, promise, product entry
Opens on restrained black/white identity cards (“2.0”, “Introducing Manus 2.0”, “See”), then a black chapter index with blue selection against dim neighbors. Scenic waterfall image bridges the sparse type into a translucent product/results card. White feature cards (“Fewer tokens”, “Unlock more with Manus Studio”, “Create videos”) give way to a prompt surface and dark creative workspace. Hierarchy remains simple: one statement or product proof at once. Interface details are small, but the headline carries the cut.

### 20–40 — Product controls become made things
Feature language moves into game-builder controls and a playable scene. Asset choices and edit-panel state changes show interaction rather than a static feature list. A fox-like character/editor demonstration makes “edit every detail” tangible. UI-to-output alternation supplies the rhythm: choose → see result → adjust. The montage does not need noisy glitch effects; state change and framing are enough.

### 40–60 — Outcome variety and multiplayer
Clean white copy cards (“Build online / Multiplayer”, “Build online / Multiplayer games”, “Build any game / You Imagine”) separate game lobby/racing footage and different game worlds. The typography acts as a reset between richer proof shots. Bright content appears inside a controlled framing; text remains high-contrast and large.

### 60–80 — Always-on desktop workflow
Game scenes give way to cards about 3D creation, Windows apps and hosted apps. The sequence introduces a mostly white Manus Studio desktop, configuration state, prompt entry, then a vivid Windows desktop and application. Composition moves from feature promise to full interface proof. White UI is spatially organized, not over-decorated.

### 80–100 — Product demonstrations and launch phrase
The UI sequence advances from desktop preview into app interaction. A light product section is followed by a plain “Put it…” title pause, then Studio setup and computer use. The desktop/video workbench expands; a demo preview grows from small to large. Framing and scale shifts provide momentum while the UI remains readable.

### 100–120 — Computer use and character reveal
A title card (“Watch Manus use your computer”) sets up phone/computer interaction, Xcode/app testing and desktop/device states. “One More Thing” is repeated before a black chapter card with electric-blue “New” and a highlighted menu item. A compact “Meet Cue!” pill then reveals a cast of colorful geometric agent characters. The high contrast section card is a particularly effective pivot from product proof to new identity.

### 120–140 — Identity, collaboration, call handling
Agent identity begins in a scenic, expansive frame and quickly resolves to a phone UI showing a named Health Manager and its connected assets. A title reset introduces agent-to-agent collaboration. Selection checkmarks, a group chat and distinct agent messages demonstrate coordination; another white title card introduces call forwarding, followed by profile/settings and an example response. Repeated device framing creates continuity; selected color accents clarify state.

### 140–160 — QR entry point, agent utility, ecosystem
A dark QR statement contrasts with warm, lightly blurred contextual footage. A QR card becomes a friendly red mascot, resolves to a simple logo, and enters a white product UI. “Saved to your team” uses blue emphasis beside a legible roster. “Later, from anywhere” changes setting, and a pickup request progresses to completed state. An illustrated town expands the example into a network of local agents. This builds from one assistant to an ecosystem, with copy carried by large readable phrases.

### 160–166.464 — Founder/user agency and quiet logo hold
A pale closing phrase (“Manus gives you the power to build”) elevates one blue word; the thought resolves through “You” into “You decide what’s worth building.” Characters enter from frame edges around a small line mark, then the final Manus wordmark settles into a quiet hold. The ending decelerates rather than ending on a chaotic burst.

## Cross-cutting visual grammar
- **Type:** contemporary sans-serif, usually one bold phrase at a time; restrained line lengths, ample negative space, occasional blue word emphasis. Titles are used as clean chapter punctuation.
- **Palette:** predominantly white/near-white and charcoal/black; electric blue is used selectively for selection, product emphasis and identity. Scenic blue/teal imagery is a bridge, not a reason to overload every frame.
- **UI framing:** centered cards, desktop windows and phone mockups, consistent rounded corners, fine neutral borders, readable selected states. Close-ups are earned by interaction.
- **Motion inferred from changing states:** scale/framing progression, interface state changes, text reveals and geometric/character entrances. The provided contact sheets are stills; exact easing, cut counts per bar and temporal interpolation cannot responsibly be inferred from them.
- **Pacing:** alternates dense product evidence with spare typographic breaths. Repeated title/card motifs let the viewer parse rapid feature changes without a permanent HUD cluttering the screen.
- **NewEra adaptation:** borrow the minimalist title → proof → title cadence, not Manus wording, logos, character artwork, interface or footage. NewEra uses its own mint/turquoise/periwinkle system and accurate claims.
